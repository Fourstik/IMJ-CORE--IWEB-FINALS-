Essential Computer Hardware

Best Setup:
Use a smaller SSD (250-500GB) for your OS and main programs, then add a larger HDD (1-2TB) for files, games, and media storage.

Key Features:
➢ SSD speed dramatically improves boot times
➢ NVMe interface is faster than SATA
➢ HDD provides bulk storage at lower cost
➢ Storage capacity measured in GB/TB

Permanent storage for your operating system, programs, and files. SSDs are faster but more expensive, while HDDs offer more storage for less cost.
