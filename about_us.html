About Us

We are a team of learners and tech enthusiasts who believe that understanding computer hardware doesn't have to be complicated. IMJ-CORE was built to make PC knowledge simple, clear, and accessible to anyone — whether you're completely new to computers or just looking to strengthen your basics.

We know that diving into the world of computers can feel overwhelming at first. Technical terms, endless parts, and confusing explanations often scare people away. That's why we're here: to break it all down into something anyone can follow.

At IMJ-CORE, we break down the importance of each computer component in clear and simple language. From the CPU to the power supply, we explain what each part does and why it matters, without the confusing technical jargon. Our goal is to help beginners understand the basics of hardware so they can see how every piece fits into the bigger picture of a computer.

IMJ represents the initials of our team members, arranged in a way that sounds like the word “image.” This reflects how our website uses visuals to help visitors easily understand and visualize different PC components.
Meanwhile, Core carries a deeper meaning. The CO stands for Computer, R for Reference, and E for Education. Together, they represent our mission to serve as a reliable educational reference about computers — going straight to the core of learning and understanding each component’s function and importance.
