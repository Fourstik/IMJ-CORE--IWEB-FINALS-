Size Matters:
Buy a case slightly larger than you think you need. It makes building easier and provides better airflow. Check GPU clearance specifications before buying.

Key Features:
➢ Form factor compatibility (ATX, Micro-ATX, Mini-ITX)
➢ GPU clearance for large graphics cards
➢ Front panel connectors (USB, audio, power)
➢ Cable management space behind motherboard

Houses and protects all your components while providing airflow and accessibility. Size determines what components will fit.
